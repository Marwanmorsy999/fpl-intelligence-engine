PRE-MATCH PRESS CONFERENCE — TRANSCRIPT
Fictional Rovers FC — Friday, ahead of Saturday's Premier League fixture away at Northgate United

Manager: Good morning everyone. I'll take questions in a moment, but let me start with the team news.

Manager: Marcus Ellery is ruled out for this one. He picked up a hamstring injury in the warm-up on Tuesday and the scan on Wednesday confirmed a grade two tear. Realistically we are looking at four to six weeks, so he will miss the next four matches and we will reassess after the international break.

Reporter (Sky): Any update on Danny Okoro?

Manager: Danny trained fully with the group yesterday for the first time since the ankle problem. He came through it with no reaction, so he is available. Whether he starts is a different question — he has not played ninety minutes since August and I am not going to throw him straight back in and lose him again.

Reporter (BBC): And Tomas Beier? There were reports he was carrying something.

Manager: Tomas is a doubt. He felt his calf tighten at the end of Thursday's session, so it is touch and go. We will give him until Saturday morning and make a call then. If there is any risk at all he will not travel.

Reporter (The Athletic): Kwame Asare is suspended, isn't he?

Manager: Correct, Kwame picked up his fifth yellow of the season last weekend so he is suspended for this fixture. He is back for the following one.

Reporter (The Athletic): Does that change how you set up?

Manager: It might. We have played a 4-2-3-1 for most of the season but without Kwame in that midfield pivot I expect we will go to a 4-3-3 and ask Liam Verhoeven to sit deeper. That is a new role for Liam and it will take him a couple of games, but he has the discipline for it.

Reporter (Local Press): Does that push Rafael Duarte further forward?

Manager: Yes. The plan is to play him further forward, closer to the striker, because that is where he hurts teams. He has been wasted at the base of midfield and I take responsibility for that.

Reporter (Local Press): Who is on penalties now that Kwame is out?

Manager: Rafael takes the penalties. He has done since the Cup game and he will continue to. Danny will take the corners and Liam is on direct free kicks around the box.

Reporter (Sky): You have a Thursday night European fixture next week. Will you rotate?

Manager: I will rotate, yes, but not this weekend. This weekend is a full-strength side apart from the ones I have mentioned. Sofiane Belkacem was rested last time out precisely so that he is fresh for this one, and he will start.

Reporter (BBC): And in goal?

Manager: Owen Fitzgerald will start. Patrick Nwosu has been excellent in training but Owen is the number one and that has not changed.

Reporter (BBC): Northgate have a new manager since you last played them.

Manager: They do, and it makes preparation harder. The new manager has them pressing much higher than the previous staff did and they have gone to a back three in his three games so far. We have prepared for both shapes.

Manager: One more thing before I go — Jonah Pike is back in training after the illness that kept him out last week. He is not ready to be involved on Saturday but he will be on the bench for the midweek game.

Manager: That's it, thank you.
