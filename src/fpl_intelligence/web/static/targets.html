How this is computed

Alpha = (xPTS − position-average xPTS) × (1 − ownership). Ownership uses your league's cached rival picks when at least 3 rivals are cached, otherwise the global selected-by % (labelled). Volatility = standard deviation of the player's last-5 actual points ("recent volatility"). Ranking boosts your thinnest rostered position by up to +20%. Unaffordable targets are hidden unless you tick "show all". Any missing input renders as an honest "unavailable" chip.
