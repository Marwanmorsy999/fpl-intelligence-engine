Help & FAQ

Everything you need to read your squad like an analyst. Sources: official FPL API, BBC Sport RSS (fetched server-side), Understat xG/xA and live odds where available. All numbers are labelled with their source — nothing here hides its data age.

Frequently asked questions
What is xPTS (expected points)?
A player's projected Fantasy points for the current gameweek, blended from underlying stats (xG/xA), minutes estimates, fixture difficulty and market signals. Focus or hover the xPTS chips anywhere in the app. Values are materialized daily at 06:10 UTC, so they do not recompute per request.
What does EV mean on transfer suggestions?
Expected Value: the points gained by making this transfer versus holding, net of the hit cost when applicable. The Transfer Desk ranks by EV so a −4 hit only shows when the expected gain beats the cost.
What is “alpha”?
Alpha measures how underpriced a player is: projected output relative to price and ownership. High alpha = the market hasn't priced the player's fixtures/form yet. Shown as a chip on players you own plus the Targets page.
How fresh is the live feed?
The Live page polls every 90 seconds during kickoff windows. If every upstream provider stalls we serve the last stored snapshot and say so — the age chip shows exactly how old the data is, and anything older than 90 minutes gets an explicit stale banner instead of pretending to be live.
Where are my numbers computed?
Predictions are precomputed ("materialize, don't compute per request") into read-model tables, which keeps every page under two seconds. Your session/squad stays keyed by your own FPL entry id. See the Sources page for per-source status and ages.
Something looks broken — what should I try first?
Every card now has bounded timeouts: after ~6 seconds a failed section shows an honest error with a Retry button instead of spinning forever. Retry first; if a whole page misbehaves press Refresh now / hard-reload. Persistent issues: check /sources for provider outages, then the project README issue tracker.
