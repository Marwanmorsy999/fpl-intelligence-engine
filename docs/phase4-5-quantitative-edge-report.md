## 3. Important synthetic-data limitation


The five available seasons come from `MockHistoricalDataProvider`. Points and minutes are generated from deterministic periodic formulas (e.g. points patterned by `gameweek mod 10`, minutes alternating 0/90). This is not real football data and does not exhibit realistic player variance, fixture difficulty, ownership behaviour, or form/build-order structure.

**Therefore:**
- Model results here are **pipeline validation only**.
- They must NOT be interpreted as establishing real FPL predictive edge.
- Under **STEP 11** the recommendation prioritises integrating a real historical data source before any predictive-edge claims.

## 4. Data realism audit

The mock generator may favour certain model assumptions. Potential circularity / realism issues identified:

- **Synthetic points from periodic formula**: FPL points are generated as `max(0, gw mod 10 - player_idx mod 3)` — a purely periodic signal repeating across the season and identical across seasons, NOT derived from football performance, form, or opponent quality. Any apparent 'predictive' skill of form baselines here is an artifact of this synthetic periodicity.

- **Synthetic minutes alternate 0/90**: minutes are `90 if (gw + player_idx) mod 2 == 0 else 0`. The MinutesModel's near-perfect start-probability calibration (ECE ~ 0) reflects this deterministic binary signal, not real rotation/lineup dynamics.

- **Fixtures independent of strength**: fixture scores are `(fixture_index mod 5)` / `(2*fixture_index mod 4)`, independent of any team-strength concept. Match-model expected-goals error therefore cannot show real strength-based skill.

- **Ownership/price synthetic**: selected_by_percent and price are linear functions of player index; they do not reflect real market behaviour.

**Consequence**: results whose assumptions overlap these synthetic generators are marked **'Pipeline validation only'** and must not be read as predictive edge.
